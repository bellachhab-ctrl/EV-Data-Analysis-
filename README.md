# EV Data Analysis
An interactive Tableau dashboard analysing 150,000+ electric vehicle registration records, exploring EV adoption trends throughout the years, technology and manufacturing Hubs/Countries.

**Project Background**
This Project started as a guided tutorial covering the core KPIs visualised below and was then extended with an original analysis comparing the total of electric vehicles against each manufacturer's primary EV Production Plant Country.<br>


**Project Scope:**
**KPIs**<br>
  *Total Vehicles (BEV+PHEV combined market size)<br>
  *Average Electric Range (Technology and efficiency indicator)<br>
  *Total BEV Vehicles and percentage of total<br>
  *Total PHEV Vehicles and percentage of total<br>


**Charts**<br>
  *Total Vehicles by Model Year (2011 onward) - Area Chart showing adoption growth and average trend<br>
  *Top 10 Vehicles by Make - Bar chart of car manufacturer market share<br>
  *Total Vehicles by Model - Grid view of Top Models their total and percentage<br>
  *Electric Range by car Model - Median range comparison across different Models<br>
  *Total Vehicles by Producing Country - Donut chart mapping vehicle registration to each manufacturers primary EV production Plant/Country<br>


**Tools Used**<br>
  *Tableau Public - Dashboard build, joins, visualization<br>
  *Excel - Original Dataset source<br>


**How to View**<br>
